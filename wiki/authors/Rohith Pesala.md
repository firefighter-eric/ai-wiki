## 简介

这里连接 Rohith Pesala 相关的表格研究，重点是训练数据、网格结构和模型评价怎样对应。

## 从哪里开始读

- [Smock, Pesala, Abraham - 2022 - GriTS Grid table similarity metric for table structure recognition](../summaries/Smock,%20Pesala,%20Abraham%20-%202022%20-%20GriTS%20Grid%20table%20similarity%20metric%20for%20table%20structure%20recognition.md)：待精读：GriTS 直接以表格网格比较预测与真实结构，研究比单纯比较标记字符串更贴近表格形态的指标。
- [Smock, Pesala, Abraham - 2022 - PubTables-1M Towards comprehensive table extraction from unstructured documents](../summaries/Smock,%20Pesala,%20Abraham%20-%202022%20-%20PubTables-1M%20Towards%20comprehensive%20table%20extraction%20from%20unstructured%20documents.md)：待精读：PubTables-1M 提供大规模表格抽取标注，重点是完整、清楚的单元格结构，支撑检测和表格恢复研究。
- [Smock, Pesala, Abraham - 2023 - Aligning Benchmark Datasets for Table Structure Recognition](../summaries/Smock,%20Pesala,%20Abraham%20-%202023%20-%20Aligning%20Benchmark%20Datasets%20for%20Table%20Structure%20Recognition.md)：待精读：这篇工作对齐表格基准中的错误与不一致标注，说明评测数据的处理方式也会影响模型比较。
