## 从哪里开始读

- [OpenAI et al. - 2019 - Dota 2 with Large Scale Deep Reinforcement Learning](../summaries/OpenAI%20et%20al.%20-%202019%20-%20Dota%202%20with%20Large%20Scale%20Deep%20Reinforcement%20Learning.md)：待精读：OpenAI Five 把已有强化学习方法扩大到复杂的 Dota 2 环境，研究长时间决策、部分可见信息与大规模训练。
- [Ouyang et al. - 2022 - Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback](../summaries/Ouyang%20et%20al.%20-%202022%20-%20Training%20language%20models%20to%20follow%20instructions%20with%20human%20feedback.md)：InstructGPT 先学习示范，再学习人类偏好并做强化学习，研究让语言模型更符合用户意图。
- [Wu et al. - 2021 - Recursively Summarizing Books with Human Feedback](../summaries/Wu%20et%20al.%20-%202021%20-%20Recursively%20Summarizing%20Books%20with%20Human%20Feedback.md)：待精读：这篇整本书摘要工作把长任务递归拆成小摘要，再用人类反馈改善各层结果，研究超长材料怎样逐步压缩。
