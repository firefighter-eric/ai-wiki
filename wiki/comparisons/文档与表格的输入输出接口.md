# 文档与表格的输入输出接口

## TL;DR（快速导读）

页面转写、表格结构恢复、单表问答和电子表格压缩需要不同表示。先决定保留哪些行列、坐标、样式与运算，再选模型；结构合法和答案正确要分别验收。

## 四种问题不能混成一张排行榜

| 问题 | 输入与输出 | 主要误差 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 页面转换 | 页面图像 → 文本/结构标签 | 顺序、漏块、结构丢失 |
| 表格恢复 | 页面/表格图像 → 行列与跨度 | 合并格、表头、内容错位 |
| 表格问答 | 表和问题 → 单元格/聚合/答案 | 选格、运算和语义错误 |
| 电子表格压缩 | 原生表 → 紧凑文本表示 | 样式、位置和细节丢失 |

同一个数字出现在正确单元格、正确行列关系里，才能继续计算；字符都正确仍可能给出错误汇总。

## 表格结构与表格推理

[TAPAS](../summaries/Herzig%20et%20al.%20-%202020%20-%20TaPas%20Weakly%20Supervised%20Table%20Parsing%20via%20Pre-training.md)在 BERT 中增加表格位置表示，以弱监督学习单元格和聚合；原范围是可放入内存的单表，不是任意多表数据库。[表格文本 TableFormer](../summaries/Yang%20et%20al.%20-%202021%20-%20Robust%20Transformer%20Modeling%20for%20Table-Text%20Encoding.md)使用结构偏置减轻顺序扰动，但严格不变性也可能失去问题依赖的行序。这里的 TableFormer 不应与同名视觉表格识别工作混淆。

[OTSL](../summaries/Lysak%20et%20al.%20-%202023%20-%20Optimized%20Table%20Tokenization%20for%20Table%20Structure%20Recognition.md)用少量符号描述二维网格，非法序列可检查，合法序列仍可能错误。[LORE](../summaries/Xing%20et%20al.%20-%202023%20-%20LORE%20Logical%20Location%20Regression%20Network%20for%20Table%20Structure%20Recognition.md)与[LORE++](../summaries/Long%20et%20al.%20-%202024%20-%20LORE%20Logical%20Location%20Regression%20Network%20for%20Table%20Structure%20Recognition%20with%20Pre-training.md)回归逻辑位置；[PubTables-1M](../summaries/Smock%2C%20Pesala%2C%20Abraham%20-%202022%20-%20PubTables-1M%20Towards%20comprehensive%20table%20extraction%20from%20unstructured%20documents.md)的数据与 canonicalization 处理则影响结构评价。结构、内容和下游答案是三项需要分别核对的结果。

## 文档模型与压缩的前提

[DiT 文档预训练](../summaries/Li%20et%20al.%20-%202022%20-%20DiT%20Self-supervised%20Pre-training%20for%20Document%20Image%20Transformer.md)学习文档视觉表示，[DocTr](../summaries/Liao%20et%20al.%20-%202023%20-%20DocTr%20Document%20Transformer%20for%20Structured%20Information%20Extraction%20in%20Documents.md)针对结构化信息抽取，二者都不是通用无条件全文转写器。[SpreadsheetLLM](../summaries/Tian%20et%20al.%20-%202024%20-%20SpreadsheetLLM%20Encoding%20Spreadsheets%20for%20Large%20Language%20Models.md)压缩结构锚点、重复信息和数据格式以控制 token，但原方案没有充分利用颜色与边框。压缩后能回答一组问题，不代表原表所有公式、样式和语义均可逆恢复。

[VRAG-RL](../summaries/Wang%20et%20al.%20-%202025%20-%20VRAG-RL%20Empower%20Vision-Perception-Based%20RAG%20for%20Visually%20Rich%20Information%20Understanding%20via%20Iterative%20Reasoning%20wit.md)将视觉检索与迭代推理连接；搜索工具、证据页和奖励条件共同决定效果。[Seed1.5-VL](../summaries/Seed%20-%20Unknown%20-%20Seed1.5-VL%20Technical%20Report.md)报告视觉与视频理解能力及局限，不能凭通用 VL 标签替代文档或表格专门测试。本文的取舍是保留原始页面或原生表作为复核来源，转写与压缩只作为有损中间表示。

## 证据基础

- [TAPAS](../summaries/Herzig%20et%20al.%20-%202020%20-%20TaPas%20Weakly%20Supervised%20Table%20Parsing%20via%20Pre-training.md)：单表选择与聚合。

- [LORE++](../summaries/Long%20et%20al.%20-%202024%20-%20LORE%20Logical%20Location%20Regression%20Network%20for%20Table%20Structure%20Recognition%20with%20Pre-training.md)：预训练扩展。
- [PubTables-1M](../summaries/Smock%2C%20Pesala%2C%20Abraham%20-%202022%20-%20PubTables-1M%20Towards%20comprehensive%20table%20extraction%20from%20unstructured%20documents.md)：数据与表结构评价。
- [DiT](../summaries/Li%20et%20al.%20-%202022%20-%20DiT%20Self-supervised%20Pre-training%20for%20Document%20Image%20Transformer.md)：文档图像预训练。
- [DocTr](../summaries/Liao%20et%20al.%20-%202023%20-%20DocTr%20Document%20Transformer%20for%20Structured%20Information%20Extraction%20in%20Documents.md)：结构化文档抽取。
- [SpreadsheetLLM](../summaries/Tian%20et%20al.%20-%202024%20-%20SpreadsheetLLM%20Encoding%20Spreadsheets%20for%20Large%20Language%20Models.md)：电子表格压缩。
