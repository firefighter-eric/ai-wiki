# 语音表示、识别与合成

## TL;DR（快速导读）

语音识别把音频变文字，TTS 把文字变音频，声码器把声学表示变波形。自监督表示和音视频输入可以帮助其中部分阶段；它们的质量与训练条件不能互相替代。

## 简介

这页按输入输出串起已收录的语音来源，帮助读者区分表示学习、转写、声学建模、波形合成与可控重合成。它提供方法导航和证据约束，不声称所有来源在同一数据、硬件或听测协议下可比。

## 关键属性

| 阶段 | 输入 → 输出 | 对应评价 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 表示学习 | 音频/音视频 → 隐藏表示 | 迁移任务及标注量 |
| ASR / AVSR | 音频/嘴部视频 → 文字 | WER，按噪声与语言分开 |
| 声学模型 | 文字 → mel/其他声学表示 | 自然度、时长和发音控制 |
| 声码器 | 声学表示 → 波形 | 听测、失真与实时性 |
| 重合成 | 内容、F0、说话人 → 波形 | 重建、控制与身份保持 |

一套 TTS 听测成绩同时受声学模型和声码器影响；不能把它全部归因给文本到 mel 的模块。

## 相关主张

[AV-HuBERT](../summaries/Ai%20-%202022%20-%20Learning%20Audio-Visual%20Speech%20Representation%20by%20Masked%20Multimodal%20Cluster%20Prediction.md)利用音频与嘴部运动的相关性学习隐藏单元，预训练不用转写标签，但下游识别仍需按标注量比较。[抗噪 AVSR](../summaries/Ai%20-%20Unknown%20-%20Robust%20Self-Supervised%20Audio-Visual%20Speech%20Recognition.md)在噪声和干扰说话人场景使用视觉线索，收益依噪声类别与 SNR；英语 LRS3 结果不等于任意语言或遮脸场景成立。

[Tacotron](../summaries/Wang%20et%20al.%20-%202017%20-%20Tacotron%20Towards%20end-To-end%20speech%20synthesis.md)与[Tacotron 2](../summaries/Shen%20et%20al.%20-%202018%20-%20Natural%20TTS%20Synthesis%20by%20Conditioning%20Wavenet%20on%20MEL%20Spectrogram%20Predictions.md)组织文字、mel 与波形生成，[FastPitch](../summaries/%C5%81a%20-%20Unknown%20-%20FASTPITCH%20PARALLEL%20TEXT-TO-SPEECH%20WITH%20PITCH%20PREDICTION%20Adrian%20%C5%81a%C2%B4%20ncucki%20NVIDIA%20Corporation.md)引入音高预测及并行结构。FastPitch 论文用 WaveGlow 产生波形，不能把输出音质解释成它单独完成。[MelGAN](../summaries/Kumar%20et%20al.%20-%202019%20-%20MelGAN%20Generative%20adversarial%20networks%20for%20conditional%20waveform%20synthesis.md)与[HiFi-GAN](../summaries/Kong%2C%20Kim%2C%20Bae%20-%202020%20-%20HiFi-GAN%20Generative%20adversarial%20networks%20for%20efficient%20and%20high%20fidelity%20speech%20synthesis.md)主要是波形合成路线，输入与文字识别不同。

[离散表示语音重合成](../summaries/Polyak%20et%20al.%20-%202021%20-%20Speech%20resynthesis%20from%20discrete%20disentangled%20self-supervised%20representations.md)拆分内容、F0 和说话人身份；可控制某个因子不表示因子完全独立，也不保证分布外操作自然。本文较稳定的综合判断是先检查发音正确，再检查听感、身份和控制；单一 MOS 或 WER 不覆盖全部目标。

## 来源支持

- [AV-HuBERT](../summaries/Ai%20-%202022%20-%20Learning%20Audio-Visual%20Speech%20Representation%20by%20Masked%20Multimodal%20Cluster%20Prediction.md)：音视频隐藏单元与标注量。
- [Robust AVSR](../summaries/Ai%20-%20Unknown%20-%20Robust%20Self-Supervised%20Audio-Visual%20Speech%20Recognition.md)：噪声/SNR 条件。
- [Tacotron](../summaries/Wang%20et%20al.%20-%202017%20-%20Tacotron%20Towards%20end-To-end%20speech%20synthesis.md)：文字到声学表示。
- [Tacotron 2](../summaries/Shen%20et%20al.%20-%202018%20-%20Natural%20TTS%20Synthesis%20by%20Conditioning%20Wavenet%20on%20MEL%20Spectrogram%20Predictions.md)：mel 与 WaveNet 波形阶段。
- [FastPitch](../summaries/%C5%81a%20-%20Unknown%20-%20FASTPITCH%20PARALLEL%20TEXT-TO-SPEECH%20WITH%20PITCH%20PREDICTION%20Adrian%20%C5%81a%C2%B4%20ncucki%20NVIDIA%20Corporation.md)：并行音高控制及声码器条件。
- [MelGAN](../summaries/Kumar%20et%20al.%20-%202019%20-%20MelGAN%20Generative%20adversarial%20networks%20for%20conditional%20waveform%20synthesis.md)：对抗式波形生成。
- [HiFi-GAN](../summaries/Kong%2C%20Kim%2C%20Bae%20-%202020%20-%20HiFi-GAN%20Generative%20adversarial%20networks%20for%20efficient%20and%20high%20fidelity%20speech%20synthesis.md)：声码器训练与质量。
- [语音重合成](../summaries/Polyak%20et%20al.%20-%202021%20-%20Speech%20resynthesis%20from%20discrete%20disentangled%20self-supervised%20representations.md)：内容、F0 与身份因子。
